Promoboard design rules

What our in-house designer needs from you to design your Moodus board.

What we need from you

We start designing once the required items are in. Anything missing pauses your board.

  • LogoRequired

  • TextRequired

  • Board modelRequired

  • Brand coloursIf you have them

  • FontsIf you have them

  • PhotosIf you have them

  • QR codeIf you have them

  • Style directionIf you have them

  • One revision round

    You receive a design proposal, and you get one round of corrections on it. Send all your remarks together in a single message. Each additional revision round is €25.

  • Send it to us

    Send everything in one email to design@moodus.com.

What we cannot use

These look fine on a screen but fall apart at print size. If this is all you have, let us know and we will tell you what is possible.

  • A logo copied or downloaded from a website
  • Screenshots, or photos of a printed logo
  • Images sent through WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram
  • A logo pasted into Word, PowerPoint or a PDF
  • Images under 300 dpi at the real board size

What is included

The design process covers the design itself. It does not cover correcting or improving the material you send us.

Included

Layout

Composition, sizing and balance across the whole board.

Bleed & safe zone

10 mm bleed around the board, 10 mm safe margin inside it.

Cutting contour

The cut line and the terminal opening, positioned exactly.

Print file

Colour conversion and a production-ready file for the printer.

Not included

  • Checking your text for spelling or typing errors
  • Retouching or editing your images
